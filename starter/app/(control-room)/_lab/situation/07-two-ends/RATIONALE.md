# situation 07: Two ends

- References: Fletes shipment detail view, a single arc between two pins on a pale map with the two place cards ("Punto de salida", "Punto de entrega") side by side under it (https://dribbble.com/shots/6929918-Shipment-Detail-View, `research/premium/visual/shots/situation-04-fletes-arc-route.png`); Fastship's dashed origin-to-destination connector (`research/premium/visual/shots/situation-01-fastship-shipment-details.png`).
- Borrowed: the dotted arc instead of a straight road line, the muted map, and the two ends as two columns joined to the map's foot, pickup left and delivery right.
- For the ops person: an arc reads at once as "from here to there" and does not pretend a road route is planned when nothing is booked; the two columns put the two places and their times next to each other, so a missing time on one side shows as an empty-looking cell.
- Trade-off: two columns halve the width for long names ("Private address, Hamburg" wraps); the arc is less exact than today's line about which way the car goes.
