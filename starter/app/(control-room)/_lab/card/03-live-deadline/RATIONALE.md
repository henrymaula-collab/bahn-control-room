# card 03: Live deadline

- Reference: Apple HIG Live Activities (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities, raw copy `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-live-activities.md`): for tasks with a defined end, "focus on important information that people need to see at a glance", "use large, heavier-weight text", and animate to show an update. Flighty's countdown to departure as a live number (`research/premium/visual/shots/card-13-flighty-was-now.png`).
- Borrowed: the deadline as its own glanceable platter at Headline weight with the time left beside it in tabular figures, and a 3 px bar that drains through the last hour; the minutes tick down with the clock while the card is open.
- Why for ops: "Decide by 12:30" asks the reader to do arithmetic against the clock; "30 min left" with a half-empty red bar does not. Red appears only inside the hour (the app's own rule), so it keeps meaning danger.
- The card under it is today's: the situation and the question as two blocks on the ramp.
- Trade-off: the platter adds one surface to the page; the room's clock is the dataset's 12:00, so "left" is counted from it plus the real minutes since the card opened.
