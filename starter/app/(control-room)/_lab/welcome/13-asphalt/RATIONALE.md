# welcome 13: Asphalt panel

- References: Bahn's own driver app, an asphalt page with grey-950 cards and a birch pill as the primary button (`design/brand-imagery/drivers-screen-1-v1.png`, `design/brand-imagery/drivers-screen-2-v1.png`, described in `design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`); bahnexpress.com's stat strip, grey-950 at 95 % with birch type (https://www.bahnexpress.com, `design/screens/com-home-desktop-1440-fold.png`).
- Borrowed: Bahn's dark surface and its inverted button, exactly their tokens: grey-950 (solid: at the site strip's 95 % the map's labels showed through), birch text, grey-400 for second lines, the birch pill, red-400 for a deadline within the hour, the brighter green-500 dots of their dark map.
- Why for ops: the panel is the darkest thing on a light map, so the eye lands on it first and the map reads as the background; the dark surface is already Bahn's (their drivers use it every day), not a theme we invented.
- Contrast measured on #1e1c17: birch 16.8:1, grey-400 5.9:1, red-400 6.2:1 (all above Apple's 4.5:1).
- Trade-off: a dark block next to the light side menu splits the frame in two tones; the rest of the app stays light, so this panel is the only dark surface.
