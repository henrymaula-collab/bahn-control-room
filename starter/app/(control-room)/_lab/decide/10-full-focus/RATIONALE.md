# decide 10: Full focus

**Reference**: HEY Focus & Reply, "line them up, then one after another", https://www.hey.com/features/ (board `research/premium/product/needs.md` 14); Superhuman, act and move to the next, https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005833597709-Achieve-Inbox-Zero, shot `research/premium/product/shots/needs-05-superhuman-get-me-to-zero.png`; Apple HIG motion (brief, cancellable, replace movement with fades under Reduce Motion) in `research/premium/product/rules.md` sections 1 and 6.

**What it borrows**: HEY's focus mode, one message at a time with nothing else on screen, and its lined-up queue afterwards; Superhuman's "the next one is the default". Here the email takes the whole page frame, set like a letter at reading width with the German one step up the type ramp; In English swaps the text in place and says it still goes out in German; Esc goes back. Send lifts the letter away and a slim strip at the top of the card holds Undo for 10 s. After the decision the card says what is left ("2 cases left"), the next one lifted, and the primary button names it ("Open the Volvo V60").

**Why it helps**: the one moment where a person signs off words in a language they may not read gets their full attention, and the moment after answers the only question left ("what now?") with the line itself.

**Trade-off**: the situation is out of sight while reading; the done view drops the decided case's headline for the queue.
