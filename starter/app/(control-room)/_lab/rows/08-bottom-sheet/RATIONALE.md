# rows 08: Other options rise from the bottom

- Reference: Apple HIG Sheets (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets, `research/premium/rule/shots/rows-hig-sheets.png`): two detents, medium (about half) for progressive disclosure and large; a grabber in a resizable sheet; "keep sheet interactions brief". Material 3 bottom sheets (https://m3.material.io/components/bottom-sheets/guidelines, `research/premium/product/raw/rules/m3-bottom-sheets.md`).
- Borrowed: Other options rises from the bottom of the card's column (not the whole window) at the medium detent: the step, what it changes, the reasons as one-tap buttons, one full-width row each as in today's sheet (reviewer RV7: wrapped bubbles of mixed width read as chips); the card's question stays visible above under a light scrim. It grows to the large detent when there is more to read (the message it sends, your own words) or when the grabber is pressed.
- Why for ops: the answer the person is overriding stays in sight while they say why; the common case (pick a reason) never needs the large sheet.
- Trade-off: a bottom sheet is an iPhone gesture on a desktop; at Henry's laptop size the medium detent is about 280 px, so a long reason list scrolls.
