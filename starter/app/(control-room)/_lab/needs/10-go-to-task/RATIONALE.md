# needs 10: Go to a task, Command K

What it is: Command K (Control K off a Mac) opens a palette in the upper third over any page, the Overview too. Type a car, a pickup place, an order number or a time; the first match is ready and Enter opens it; arrows move; results are grouped by time; the keys are taught once at the foot. The card's row reads "Other tasks  36  ⌘K" and opens the same palette with a click.

Borrowed from: Linear's command palette (https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/7434ce1f-0ec6-45cb-9a44-10269b2e55c2, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-10-linear-command-palette.png`): upper third, page dimmed lightly, a no-match row that offers the next step (here: search every order in Orders). Chatwoot's "Search or jump to" (`research/premium/visual/shots/needs-08-chatwoot-jump-palette.png`): small group names and the key legend at the foot. Raycast's action panel (https://manual.raycast.com/action-panel, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/raycast-actionpanel.md`): Enter runs the first item.

Why it helps under time pressure: with 37 tasks, typing "volvo" or "rivel" beats scrolling, and the list never takes room on the card. The five BMW M4s are told apart by typing their place.

Trade-off: a palette is invisible until summoned; a newcomer learns it from the row's "⌘K". Command K is taken from the browser's own search shortcut where one exists.
