# rows 04: Other options as an action list

- Reference: Raycast Action Panel (https://manual.raycast.com/action-panel, `research/premium/rule/shots/rows-raycast-action-panel.png`): the actions for the selected item in a compact list with the shortcut on the right, Enter runs, Esc closes. Superhuman Command (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005701270541-Keyboard-Shortcuts-in-Superhuman-Mail): keys are learned from the list, not printed on the screen.
- Borrowed: Other options opens as a floating list anchored to its button (no scrim), each step with its consequence in grey and a keycap (1, 2, 3) on the right; arrows and Enter, or the number. Picking one turns the same panel into its reasons, numbered the same way, so a decision with a reason is two keystrokes. Escape steps back, then closes.
- Why for ops: the expert who disagrees with the agent never leaves the keyboard or the card; the newcomer sees the same list and simply clicks.
- Trade-off: a popover with a second step is denser than today's sheet; the email draft for an alternative sits behind a small disclosure inside it.
