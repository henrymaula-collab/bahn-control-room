# menu 04: The next deadline under Your tasks

References: Things 3, counts only where something waits (https://culturedcode.com/things/, `research/premium/visual/shots/menu-01-things-lists.png`); Superhuman split counts, hidden at zero (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005619081101-Default-Split-Inbox, `research/premium/product/shots/needs-04-superhuman-split.png`); Flighty's status lines that give the time that matters (https://flighty.com/, `research/premium/product/map.md` 06).

Borrowed: the count stays where it is; under the name, one line in the menu's small size names the next car and when it must be decided ("BMW M4 by 12:30"), red within the hour like every other deadline in the app.

Why it helps: from any page (Orders, Know-how, the Overview) the person sees how close the next deadline is without opening Your tasks, which is the one thing that should pull them back. One line, never a list, so it works the same with 3 tasks or 40 (Henry: the nested list fails with many).

Trade-off: the Your tasks row is taller than the others, and the line repeats what the welcome panel shows on the Overview.
