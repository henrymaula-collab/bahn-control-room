# needs 01: Previous and Next at the foot

Borrows from an expense review drawer by Oyelaja Theophilus (https://dribbble.com/shots/27549633-Expense-Review-Detail-Drawer-for-Finance-Managers, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-11-expense-drawer-prev-next.png`) and Stripe Radar's review queue (https://docs.stripe.com/radar/reviews, detailed view with Previous / Next and J / K).
What it takes: Previous and Next live at the foot of the open item, under its decision, so the queue is walked from inside the case; Stripe's "a deciding action removes the item, so the count shrinks".
Here: one pinned line under the card, the previous car on the left in grey, "2 of 12" in the middle, the next car and its decide-by time on the right; it stays at the bottom edge while the card scrolls. The side menu keeps only "Your tasks N", so 3, 12 and 37 tasks look the same. After a decision, "Next: Volvo V60" is the one black button with its deadline under it.
Why for an ops person under time pressure: the next step is always in the same place, with the car named, so moving on costs no search and no list; J and K do the same from the keyboard.
Trade-off: you only see your neighbours, never the whole queue; jumping to the eighth task means stepping or the menu count. Pair it with a list variant (N2) for the long tail.
