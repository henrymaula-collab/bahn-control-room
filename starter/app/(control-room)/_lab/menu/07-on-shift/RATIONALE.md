# menu 07: Who is on shift with you

References: Steep's person block with the account and its people (https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/3e34fe50-979f-4fee-93d1-5b90887263ed, `research/premium/visual/shots/menu-05-steep-workspaces.png`); Bahn's portal account menu (round photos, already the model for today's block, `app/(control-room)/shell.css` `.avatar`); DESIGN_V3 decision 14, Team shows "on shift now".

Borrowed: the person block grows one row up: the faces of the others on shift (the switch's own list, `people`), overlapping like a stack of photos, the first five then "+N", one tap to Team. The signed-in person stays as today: photo, name, job title in Archivo italic, the switch.

Why it helps: Bahn's careers page says "No handovers, no silos" (design/BAHN-VOCABULARY.md); knowing who else is in the room right now is what lets a person ask a colleague before deciding something new alone. It also puts the team's portraits on screen in the video's first frame.

Trade-off: five small photos are more detail at the foot of a quiet menu; the row says nothing about what each person is doing (Team does).
