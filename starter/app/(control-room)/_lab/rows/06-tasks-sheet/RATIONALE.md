# rows 06: Other tasks as a sheet with a find field

- Reference: Apple HIG Sheets (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets, `research/premium/rule/shots/rows-hig-sheets.png`): one sheet at a time, brief, with a clear way out. WAI-ARIA listbox (https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/listbox/, `research/premium/product/raw/rules/apg-listbox.md`): arrows, Home and End move through a long list; type-ahead for more than seven items.
- Borrowed: the one place in the needs axis nobody took (PLAN-N1/N2): Other tasks opens a sheet in the same frame as Other options, so both "more" moments look alike. From nine tasks it has a find field (car, pickup place or order); the tasks are by decide-by with the time on the right, red within the hour; arrows and Enter.
- Many tasks: 37 tasks scroll inside the sheet; typing "Ferrari" or "Tervavio" narrows them at once.
- Why for ops: switching cases is a short interruption, which is what a sheet is for; the card behind stays where it was.
- Trade-off: a modal for navigation hides the card while looking; with three tasks the sheet is more than the job needs. With `?v=needs:nn` the needs variant's row takes its place.
