# needs 12: Counts in the menu, a popover beside it

What it is: the side menu never lists tasks, it counts them. Under the sections, "Needs you" shows at most three lines, Within the hour 8 (the count red), Later today 25, Tomorrow or later 4. A line opens its tasks as a popover beside the menu, over the page; the case you are on is highlighted. No row under the card. Works on every page, the Overview too.

Borrowed from: Things 3's sidebar, fixed lists with a count each (https://culturedcode.com/things/, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-16-things-today-sidebar.png`). Superhuman's split counts (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005619081101-Default-Split-Inbox, `research/premium/product/shots/needs-04-superhuman-split.png`): sections named by what they hold, a number beside each, hidden at zero. Dovetail's popover over the page (`research/premium/visual/shots/needs-05-dovetail-up-to-date.png`). Apple HIG Sidebars: "show no more than two levels of hierarchy in a sidebar" (`research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-sidebars.md`).

Why it helps under time pressure: this is Henry's own problem answered head on: the menu is exactly as tall with 37 tasks as with 3, and "8 within the hour" is the one number that matters at a glance.

Trade-off: the tasks are two clicks away instead of one, and the popover covers part of the card while it is open.
