# chat 07: The conversation as the case record

- References: Zendesk's event line between messages, "Check the weather pre-approved auto assist action completed" (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/7051314237466-Using-auto-assist-to-solve-tickets, `research/premium/product/shots/card-05-zendesk-preapproved-action-log.png`); GitHub Copilot's recorded reasons, every action "explains each change" and keeps its reason (https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/agent-capabilities/agent-powered-workflows-and-operations/about-automation-rationale-and-approvals, text only, `research/premium/product/raw/chatmap/chat-github-rationale.md`).
- Borrowed: who and when on every message, and what the agent did as a one-line event with a tick between the messages (Zendesk); the decision kept together with its reason (GitHub).
- Exactly: entries with a small head (the agent's mark or the person's initial, the name, the time), the agent's steps as grey event lines ("The agent read the case, the call with Calverona Hamburg and looked up earlier cases of this kind"), the running step with a small turning ring, and the yes as the record's last event: "Aino decided: Send the email, 12:04. Logged with this conversation as the reason. Next: the agent checks with the branch again at 10:00 ..." The field says "Ask, and it goes in the record".
- Why for ops: it is the pitch's loop on screen (the conversation, the decision and the reason are one record), and a shift lead reading it later sees who knew what when.
- Trade-off: names and times add a line per turn, so less fits at Henry's size; it reads more like History than like a conversation.
