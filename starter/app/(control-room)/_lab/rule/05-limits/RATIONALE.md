- Borrowed: the contract as label and value, the house style for facts: When, It will ("Pause the gig until the pickup place confirms. Nothing else."), A person still (every other kind, and any of these where the agent would do something else or write to the customer: `ruleFor` in lib/control-room/learning.ts), Take it back (turn it off here at any time, and these come to people again).
