# menu 02: The open section in asphalt

References: Apple HIG Sidebars, the selected row as a filled capsule in the app's accent with light type (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars, `research/premium/visual/shots/menu-mm-hig-sidebars.png`); Bahn's own black pill (the welcome panel's "3 tasks for you to get started", the portal's primary buttons; `app/(control-room)/overview.css` `.tasks-today`).

Borrowed: Apple's full-width selected capsule, in Bahn's accent, which is asphalt (#12110e), not a colour; birch type and icon on it; the count dimmed to sand.

Why it helps: "where am I" is answered by the strongest mark in the menu, and the menu shares one shape language with the welcome pill and the decision card's primary button, so the frame looks designed as one system rather than a grey default.

Trade-off: a black row competes a little with the page's own black button (Send the email); on Your tasks there are two black things on screen.
