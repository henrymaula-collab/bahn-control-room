# needs 03: Not now, until a time

Borrows from Superhuman Remind Me (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005666142733-Remind-Me), Linear's snooze (https://linear.app/docs/triage, `research/premium/product/shots/needs-03-linear-snooze.png`) and Untitled UI's command menu "Remind me" (https://dribbble.com/shots/26999542-Command-menu-Untitled-UI, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-15-untitled-cmd-remind-me.png`).
What it takes: "later" is a real answer with a return time (Superhuman's H, "H for hour to be reminded"); Linear's "until a chosen time or when there's new activity, whichever comes first"; Untitled UI's short menu of times.
Here: a line pinned to the bottom edge of the card column holds "Not now" (key H) on the left and the case that opens after it on the right. Not now opens a menu upward: two times worked out from the case's own decide-by ("Until 12:30, half an hour before you must decide", "Until 12:45") and "Until something new happens". Picking one sets the case aside and opens the next; that case's top says "Ferrari Roma is set aside. It comes back at 12:30." with Undo. Set-aside cases are listed in the same menu with their return time.
Why for an ops person under time pressure: with 12 or 37 tasks, the queue in front of you should be what can be decided now; a case waiting on a call back leaves it with a time, so nothing is lost.
Trade-off: a third choice next to the card's two buttons; and there is no engine field for "later" yet, so it lives in this browser's sessionStorage (the menu count does not drop, J and K still visit it). Adoption needs a "later until" on the case.
