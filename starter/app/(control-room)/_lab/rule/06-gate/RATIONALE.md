# rule 06: The gate as a checklist

- Reference: GitHub rule insights (https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-rulesets/managing-rulesets-for-a-repository#viewing-insights-for-rulesets, `research/premium/rule/shots/rule-04-github-rulesets.png`): each check a rule makes is one row with a quiet Pass or Fail mark. Stripe "Review new rule" (https://docs.stripe.com/radar/testing, `research/premium/rule/shots/rule-06-stripe-radar-rules.png`): the evidence on top, and the only ways out (Cancel, Edit, Add rule) at the foot of the same page. Apple HIG Privacy (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy, `research/premium/rule/shots/rule-01-apple-hig-privacy.png`): ask with one sentence that says exactly what will happen.
- Borrowed: the trust gate made visible as three met conditions: Enough answers (30 since 12 Aug, at least 16 must agree: the engine's gate), From more than one person (the four faces and names), They agree (29 of 30; opens to how answers are weighed, by each person's record on this kind and never by title, then Sofie's one different answer, the reason said most often, History). One plain tick each in ink (reviewer RV7 took out the green ring: Bahn's green token is "a car on plan, as a dot only", control-room.css, and a coloured ring reads as a status badge); Turn it on at the foot.
- Why for ops: it answers "is this safe?" with the same rules the engine uses, in words; the lead sees why the agent may ask now and not after one lucky week.
- After "Turn it on": the foot says the waiting Mercedes-Benz EQE at Arvelano Hamburg had its gig paused and left Mikko's tasks; Turn it off sits where Turn it on was.
- Trade-off: a box with ticks is the most "system" looking of the six; the people are present but small.
