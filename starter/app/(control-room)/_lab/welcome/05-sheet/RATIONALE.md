# welcome 05: A full-height sheet

- References: Flighty's live map, a list sheet beside the map that holds any number of flights (https://flighty.com, `research/premium/visual/shots/map-05-flighty-live-map.png`); Apple HIG Sheets, a resizable sheet with its own scrolling content beside a map (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-sheets.md`); Things' Today in the sidebar (`research/premium/visual/shots/needs-16-things-today-sidebar.png`).
- Borrowed: a sheet that runs the full height of the map frame, a fixed header (greeting and black pill), a list that scrolls under a soft fade, and a foot that stays (the key).
- Why for ops: today's panel runs off the bottom at 12 tasks; the sheet holds 3, 12 or 40 without changing shape, and the key is always in the same place for the eye.
- Empty: the list says what the agent is doing and that a new case comes here first.
- Trade-off: a scroll area on the Overview (today's panel avoids one on purpose); the sheet takes a fixed strip of the map even with three tasks.
