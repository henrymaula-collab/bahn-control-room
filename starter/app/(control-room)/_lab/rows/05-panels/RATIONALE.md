# rows 05: Why and Other options as the side panel

- Reference: Apple HIG Sheets (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets, `research/premium/rule/shots/rows-hig-sheets.png`): "Use a panel instead of a sheet if people need to repeatedly provide input and observe results". Material 3 side sheets (https://m3.material.io/components/side-sheets/specs, `research/premium/product/raw/rules/m3-side-sheets-specs.md`): a side sheet next to the content, up to 400dp.
- Borrowed: one panel at a time on the right, the place Talk it through already uses for the conversation. Why it came to you and Other options open there too, over the situation; no scrim, so the card's question stays readable while choosing a step and its reason. Escape or the close button brings the situation back; opening the chat closes them.
- Why for ops: one grammar for everything under the card ("it opens on the right"), and the decision is never hidden behind a modal while the person weighs it.
- Trade-off: the situation (map, facts) is covered while a panel is open; on narrow screens the panel takes the full width.
