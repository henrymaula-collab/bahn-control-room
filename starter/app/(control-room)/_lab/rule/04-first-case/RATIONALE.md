# rule 04: What changes, on the case waiting now

- Reference: GitHub rulesets in Evaluate mode (https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-rulesets/managing-rulesets-for-a-repository#viewing-insights-for-rulesets, `research/premium/rule/shots/rule-04-github-rulesets.png`): "you can see actions that would have passed or failed if the ruleset had been active". Stripe Radar rule testing (https://docs.stripe.com/radar/testing, `research/premium/rule/shots/rule-06-stripe-radar-rules.png`): the results help you "estimate its future impact".
- Borrowed: show the effect on a real target, not in the abstract. The one case of this kind waiting now (CD-420347, Mercedes-Benz EQE at Arvelano Hamburg, in Mikko's tasks) is shown as a quiet block with "Today" (Mikko reads it and decides; people took 9 minutes on these at the median) against "With the rule" (at once, the agent pauses the gig; it leaves Mikko's tasks).
- After "Turn it on": the block says it happened ("The agent paused the gig, and it left Mikko's tasks"); this is the real engine result (the case becomes handled by the agent).
- Why for ops: a shift lead weighs a rule by what it does to her team's queue today; one named case makes that concrete in a second. The evidence (faces, the one dissent, the most used reason) is one row down.
- Trade-off: when no case of the kind is waiting, the block is gone and the card is plain.
