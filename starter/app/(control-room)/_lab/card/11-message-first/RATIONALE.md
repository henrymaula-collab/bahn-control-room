# card 11: The message first

- Reference: Zendesk auto assist (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9945148867866-About-auto-assist and .../7051314237466, `research/premium/product/shots/card-03-zendesk-auto-assist-approve.png`, `card-04-zendesk-suggestion-composer.png`): the drafted reply sits where the composer would be, "Details" on the left, Edit and a filled Approve on the right.
- Borrowed: the draft itself in the card, between the question and the answer: who it goes to and how, the first four lines in the language it goes out in, "Show in English" one tap away; then Send (filled), Edit (opens the whole message beside the card to change the words), Other options.
- Why for ops: when the decision is "send this", the evidence is the words. Seeing the German text and its English before pressing Send removes the extra stop of today's preview panel; Send here saves at once.
- A case without a message (the BMW) shows what changes instead, on the same surface.
- Trade-off: the card grows by about five lines; saving from the card skips today's read-the-whole-email step, so an over-quick press sends a text read only in part (Edit is still one tap away).
