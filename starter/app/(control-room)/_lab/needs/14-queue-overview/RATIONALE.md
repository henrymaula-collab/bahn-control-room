# needs 14: The whole queue as a list view

What it is: two modes. "Other tasks  36 ›" turns the card column into the whole queue: "Your tasks", one count line ("37 tasks need you. 8 within the hour."), a quiet table (car, pickup place, decide by) grouped by time, the case you came from highlighted. A row opens its card; "Back to the Ferrari Roma" or Escape returns; one black button starts with the most urgent. The side panel steps aside while the list is shown.

Borrowed from: Stripe Radar's review queue (https://docs.stripe.com/radar/reviews, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/stripe-radar-reviews.md`): a list view to scan and a detailed view to decide. The ops exception queue (https://dribbble.com/shots/27677490, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-12-ops-exception-queue.png`): the counts on top, the list under them; here as one sentence, no tiles or sparklines. Queues by Eugenia Ho (https://dribbble.com/shots/3442387-Queues, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-14-queues-time-left.png`): one "Start review" button.

Why it helps under time pressure: at the start of a shift or after a break the person sees the shape of the afternoon in one screen and plans, then decides one card at a time.

Trade-off: a mode switch costs a moment of orientation; the card is hidden while the list is up.
