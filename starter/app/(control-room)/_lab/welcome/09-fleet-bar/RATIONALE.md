# welcome 09: The key as one bar

- References: Apple's iPhone Storage, "150.69 GB of 256 GB used" over one segmented bar with a dot legend under it (https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429, `research/premium/visual/shots/welcome-w01-apple-iphone-storage-bar.png`), and the Finder's version, where hovering a part names it (`research/premium/visual/shots/welcome-w02-apple-finder-storage-bar-hover.png`); a logistics dashboard that leads with one figure and its sentence (https://dribbble.com/shots/27831162, `research/premium/visual/shots/welcome-16-supply-chain-hey-stewart.png`).
- Borrowed: the whole in one bar, the parts in proportion with thin seams, a dot legend under it, the total said in words at the right ("312 cars"); hover a part and the rest step back.
- Live: the bar grows in on arrival and a slow light passes through the agent's part, the same rhythm as the agent's breathing dots on the map. Hovering "Needs a person" says "26 need a person, 3 of them yours".
- Why for ops: the three numbers become one picture of the day: mostly green, a sliver the agent is on, a sliver for people. The proportion is the "agents do the legwork" story without a chart.
- Trade-off: a small red share can look harmless; the counts stay printed beside each dot.
