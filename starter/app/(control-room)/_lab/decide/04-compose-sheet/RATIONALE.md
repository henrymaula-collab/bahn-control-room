# decide 04: Compose sheet

**Reference**: Apple HIG sheets and alerts, https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets and .../alerts (board `research/premium/product/decide.md` 05 and `rules.md` section 4: Cancel paired with the action, the action names its result); Profound "Published successfully", https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/15c47c1b-a0c0-47b2-b6a0-fe819336783d, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-08-profound-published-view.png`; Gmail Undo Send (product board 01).

**What it borrows**: Apple's compose sheet bar (Cancel on the left, the title in the middle, Send on the right, Cmd+Return sends) and Mail's header rows with a hairline under each ("To:", "Subject:", "Language:"); In English sits at the end of the Language row. After Send the sheet drops away and an "Undo Send" bar waits at the card's foot with the seconds left. The moment after is Profound's: one tick, one sentence, two buttons (Next case, In History), centred.

**Why it helps**: the email gets the person's whole attention for the few seconds it needs, then the card comes back calm. The centred tick is the most "done" the room ever looks, which is the beat the video needs.

**Trade-off**: a modal sheet hides the situation while reading the email (HIG prefers a panel when people act repeatedly), and the done view drops the card's own layout, so the headline moves to a grey line.
