# situation 01: Place card

- Reference: Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Maps, "Place cards" and the muted emphasis style, https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps (shots `research/premium/situation-si/shots/hig-maps-place-card-iphone-full.png`, `hig-maps-muted-appearance.png`; text `research/premium/product/raw/chatmap/map-apple-hig.md`).
- Borrowed: the place card's order (map on top, the name as the title, a one-line strip of three facts with hairlines between, then details, then "Open in Apple Maps" as a full-width row), and the muted map so the pins carry the colour. The strip's labels stay in sentence case (Apple's are caps, an AI tell under Henry's rules).
- For the ops person: the three things to know at a glance sit on one line, the blocker first (Release Missing, Car Not ready), next to who drives and how far apart the ends are; the map is the biggest thing in the panel but the quietest in colour.
- The map zooms in further than today (max 14), so the Ferrari's A and B, 0.3 km apart in Hamburg, are two pins instead of one blot.
- Trade-off: the hero map costs height; at Henry's Safari size the order row sits near the fold. The strip leaves Pickup and Delivery to the stop rows, so nothing is said twice.
