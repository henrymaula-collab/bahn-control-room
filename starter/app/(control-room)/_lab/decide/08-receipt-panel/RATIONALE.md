# decide 08: Receipt panel

**Reference**: Lightspark "Payout submitted", https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/0cdfc219-ee06-43aa-959d-822e3b22ef31, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-06-lightspark-payout-submitted.png`; Stripe Radar, a decision removes the item from the queue, https://docs.stripe.com/radar/reviews (board `product/decide.md` 07); Profound "Published successfully" with a view link, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-08-profound-published-view.png`.

**What it borrows**: Lightspark turns its side panel into the receipt: a large title ("Payout submitted"), one grey sentence with the exact amount and route, the buttons in the bottom right. Here the email panel becomes "Sending", then "Email sent": "The email in German to Kovellin Automobilhandel, by email, about Ferrari Roma, CD-420458", with Undo at the foot while it can still be held back. After the decision the receipt stays over the situation with In History and Next case (Profound's view link plus the next thing), and the agent's own call summary under it once the call-back has run. Stripe Radar's lesson is the side menu: Your tasks drops from 3 to 2 at once.

**Why it helps**: one sentence with every fact that matters (language, recipient, channel, subject) is the strongest proof that the right email went to the right customer; a person can glance and move on.

**Trade-off**: the panel's room shows the receipt instead of the situation until it is closed; the large title is the loudest type in the room for a moment.
