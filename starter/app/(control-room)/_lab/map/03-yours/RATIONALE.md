# map 03: Your cases are the big dots

References: Onfleet dispatcher dashboard (https://support.onfleet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023669612-Map-Sidebar, `research/premium/product/shots/map-02-onfleet-map-and-sidebar.png`, `map-02c-onfleet-pin-colors.png`); Apple HIG Maps, selection styling (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps, `research/premium/product/shots/map-01-apple-hig-cluster.png`).

Borrowed: Onfleet treats the sidebar list and the map pins as the same objects and styles pins by who they belong to; Apple's "outline and color variation" for what matters to this person.

Why it helps: the map holds 26 red cars but Aino has three. Her three (red[id].mine) are 14 px dots with an ink ring and a halo, the same three the welcome panel lists; everyone else's red is an 8 px dot. She sees at a glance where her work is, and the rest of the team's problems stay visible but quiet.

Trade-off: a second size of red is one more thing to learn; the ink ring is also the "open" mark in other variants, so here the open dot only grows.
