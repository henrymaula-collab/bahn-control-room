# card 16: Focus

- References: Superhuman's "Get Me To Zero" dialog (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005833597709-Achieve-Inbox-Zero, shot `research/premium/product/shots/needs-05-superhuman-get-me-to-zero.png`): one centred decision, "More options…" as text, the default on the trailing side. HEY Focus & Reply (https://www.hey.com/features/, raw `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hey-features.md`, board `research/premium/product/needs.md` 14): nothing else on screen while you answer. Apple HIG Alerts (raw `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-alerts.md`): "Always place the default button on the trailing side of a row".
- Borrowed: the question owns the screen. The situation panel steps back and the card centres in the whole work area; the question is the one large line (Title 1, semibold, balanced), the situation sits above it at body size, the default answer trails "Other options". An inspector button top right ("Situation", the macOS inspector toggle) brings the panel back and hides it again; the email and the chat open beside the card as today.
- Why for ops: under time pressure the eye has one place to land and one thing to do; the map and the facts are one tap away for the case that needs them, not on screen for the nine that do not.
- Trade-off: the situation is out of sight by default, so a person who always checks the route opens it each time; it changes the frame (`:has(.lab-card-16.c16-focus)`), not only the card.
