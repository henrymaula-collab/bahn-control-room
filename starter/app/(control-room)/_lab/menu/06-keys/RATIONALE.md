# menu 06: Go-to keys (G then a letter)

References: Linear's go-to shortcuts, G then I / M / T (https://linear.app/docs/my-issues, raw `research/premium/product/raw/pp/linear-myissues.md`, board `research/premium/product/menu.md` 04); Things 3 Type Travel (https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/2785159/, board 05); Primer keyboard-shortcut rules (https://primer.style/accessibility/patterns/keyboard-shortcuts/, raw `research/premium/product/raw/rules/primer-a11y-keyboard.md`).

Borrowed: Linear's two-key sequence (G, then O Overview, T Your tasks, H History, R Orders, A Agents, K Know-how, M Team); Primer's rules: never in a text field, never with a modifier, every row stays a plain link; macOS's habit of printing a shortcut as plain grey glyphs at the end of the row.

Why it helps: an ops person who lives in the room all day moves between Your tasks and Orders without the mouse. The key shows on hover and focus, and pressing G shows every key at once for 1.6 s, so nothing has to be learned up front. Cmd+1 to 9 were avoided: Safari uses them for tabs.

Trade-off: single letters can surprise someone typing outside a field; a real build would let people turn them off (WCAG 2.1.4).
