# menu 03: Collapses to a rail

References: Apple HIG Sidebars, "consider letting people hide the sidebar", and its sidebar toggle glyph (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars, `research/premium/visual/shots/menu-mm-hig-sidebars.png`, raw `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-sidebars.md`); Zendesk's icon rail with the product mark (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9186492658714-Using-Play-mode-to-quickly-work-through-tickets, `research/premium/product/shots/card-03-zendesk-auto-assist-approve.png`).

Borrowed: the sidebar toggle at the top of the menu; Zendesk's narrow dark rail of icons beside the work, names left out (its count badge sits on the bell in the top bar, not in the rail; here the count stays plain type).

Why it helps: at Henry's Safari window (1330 x 680 at 125%, 1064 CSS px) the full menu takes about a fifth of the width; the rail gives it back to the map on the Overview. On Your tasks the card keeps its measure, so there the room becomes air to its left. It does this by itself under 1200 px and stays full above; the toggle pins either way. Names show as a tooltip on hover or focus, the person as their photo, the count as plain type beside the Your tasks icon, as today's count.

Trade-off: at Henry's recording size the lockup becomes the mark alone and the italic job title goes, and the names need a hover, so a viewer of the video no longer reads what the room holds (Orders, Agents, Know-how, Team). needs 12's counted lines need the full menu; in the rail they step aside until the toggle opens it.
