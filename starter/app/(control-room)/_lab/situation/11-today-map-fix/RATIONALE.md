# situation 11: Today with only the map fix

- Not a new design: today's panel, unchanged, with the fix every variant carries in its map (reviewer RV5), so it can be adopted on its own.
- What it fixes: the Ferrari's A and B are 0.4 km apart in Hamburg; at today's zoom cap (12) their centres sit about 30 px apart at Henry's Safari size and the 22 px pins touch. The BMW's A sits on the "Helsinki" label ("H(A)nki") and its B on "Turku".
- The change, in `_ui/route-map.tsx` only (exact diff in `route-map.patch` beside this file): (1) `maxZoom: 12` becomes `maxZoom: 14`, so the two Hamburg pins sit about 85 px apart (the fit stops short of 14 on a 294 px map); (2) the city-name layers get `text-variable-anchor` and `text-radial-offset: 1.5`, so a name may step to another side of its point; (3) invisible symbols at each pin, at the (i) corner and along the map's edges, placed before the names, so "Helsinki" steps off the A (it lands to its left) and "Turku" under the B, and no name is drawn under a pin, under the (i) or half off the map (today's "Hamb" at the Ferrari's top edge goes too).
- Reference: Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Maps, "Cluster overlapping points of interest to improve map legibility" and annotations that never hide each other (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps, text `research/premium/product/raw/chatmap/map-apple-hig.md`). MapLibre's own collision and variable-anchor placement do the work.
- For the ops person: two ends are two pins and both cities are named, so the map answers "from where to where" at a glance, as Henry wanted when he made the panel open by default.
- Trade-off: at zoom 14 a city-centre pair shows streets, not the district; a name can still hide if no side of its point is free (it then disappears instead of running through a pin, never worse than today).
