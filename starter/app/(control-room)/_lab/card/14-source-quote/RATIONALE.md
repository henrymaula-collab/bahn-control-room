- Borrowed: one quotation under the situation, behind a thin rule, with who said it, how and when ("Calverona Hamburg, Lukas Brandt (workshop), on the call at 12:00"); the whole call (English, with times) opens in place.
- Why for ops: the situation is the agent's summary; the quote is the source. "We need the seller's signed release by e-mail ... the seller has put a hold on it" from the branch's own workshop is what lets a person trust the summary in one read.
- Taken from the case record (the agent's recorded call, or the latest first-hand message when there was no call, as on the BMW); it fades in when loaded.
- Trade-off: two more lines between the situation and the question; a quote chosen by rule can be the less telling sentence of a call.
