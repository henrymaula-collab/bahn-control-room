# needs 13: Bundled by pickup place

What it is: "Other tasks  36 ›" opens in place, grouped by pickup place. The cars waiting at the place you are on come first ("Also at Calverona: Ferrari 296 GTB 14:25"). Every other place with several cars is one row ("Rivellon  4 cars  13:05 ›") that opens to its cars; places keep deadline order, so the most urgent stays on top. Private addresses and third-party places are not one place and never bundle. With a handful of tasks the bundles are open.

Borrowed from: HEY Bundles (https://www.hey.com/features/, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hey-features.md`): "no matter how many emails they send you, they'll only take up a single row". Lightfield's tasks grouped by company (`research/premium/visual/shots/needs-02-lightfield-tasks-grouped.png`): the place is said once, in the row above its cars.

Why it helps under time pressure: one call to a branch can settle several cars at once ("each one starts a chain"); the list shows that chance where the person is already working, instead of in four separate cases.

Trade-off: with this data many places hold one car, so 36 tasks become about 28 rows, not 10; it shines when cars cluster, as at a port or a dealer group.
