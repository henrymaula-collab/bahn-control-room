# decide 05: Side by side

**Reference**: Flighty's change notices with "was", https://flighty.com, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/card-13-flighty-was-now.png` (board `visual/card.md` 13); Easymail "Sending details" before send, https://dribbble.com/shots/27485335-Easymail-Schedule-Send, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-09-sending-details-preview.png`; Gmail Undo Send (product board `decide.md` 01).

**What it borrows**: Easymail's label rows above the message; Flighty's way of stating a change: the new value in ink, "was ..." in grey under it. Here In English widens the panel over the card and sets German and English next to each other, sentence against sentence (hover a row and its English turns ink). Send leaves a dark toast at the top, "Sending the email to Kovellin Automobilhandel", with Undo and the seconds left. After the decision the card lists what changed: Pickup "Tue 27 Oct from 12:00, was Mon 26 Oct 09:00–12:00", Customer "Message sent at 12:04, in Finnish", and what the agent does next.

**Why it helps**: Aino reads Finnish and Swedish, not German; seeing each German sentence beside its English lets her check the dates and the address line by line, which is where a wrong email costs a driver. The was/now list after is the receipt in the words of the order.

**Trade-off**: the wide panel covers most of the card while it is open; the sentence pairing needs the same sentence count in both texts (it falls back to two blocks otherwise).
