# welcome 10: Listen to the day

- References: Linear Pulse, "Daily pulse for Mar 28" as the title with a Listen button beside it, then the day's items one sentence each (https://linear.app/docs/pulse, `research/premium/product/shots/welcome-01-linear-pulse-daily.png`, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/linear-pulse.md`).
- Borrowed: a dated title from the room's clock ("Wednesday 21 October") and a Listen button that reads the brief aloud.
- The brief is built from her rows: "Good afternoon, Aino. Three cars need you, all by 13:00. First, the BMW M4 at Velantora, by 12:30. Then ..." While each car is read its row lights with a thin ink bar and its pin lifts on the map, so ear, list and map say the same thing.
- Why for ops: Bahn's people are on the phone a lot; the day can be heard while they finish a call, and it fits the voice agent the product already has. In the video it is the panel that talks.
- Uses the browser's own voice (speechSynthesis), no API call; the button only appears where the browser can speak. Trade-off: a synthetic voice, and one more control in the panel.
