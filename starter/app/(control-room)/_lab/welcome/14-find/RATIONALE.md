- The field searches every car on today's map by car, pickup place, delivery place or CD number ("CD-420458", "Calverona", "Volvo"), with the state as a dot and a word; a car with a case opens in Your tasks, any other opens its order.
- Why for ops: much of the day starts with a phone call ("where is the Volvo for Velantora?"); on the Overview the answer is two keystrokes away instead of a trip to Orders.
- Trade-off: the black pill goes; the start of her work is the rows under the field, not one big button.
