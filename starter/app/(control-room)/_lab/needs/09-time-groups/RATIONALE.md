# needs 09: Time groups in place

What it is: "Other tasks  36 ›" opens in place, grouped by time. The first group (Within the hour) is open; a later group of more than four folds into one row with its count (This afternoon 23 ›), and each task is one line: the car, its pickup place in grey, the time on the right. Opening scrolls the card just enough to show the list.

Borrowed from: Things 3, Today with its quiet "This Evening" divider (https://culturedcode.com/things/, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-16-things-today-sidebar.png`): one list split by time words instead of colour. Lightfield's tasks grouped by company (https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/ecc513fb-cb1e-4733-aaff-30c5ea0cb251, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-02-lightfield-tasks-grouped.png`): a small grey group name says the shared fact once, plain one-line rows under it.

Why it helps under time pressure: at 37 tasks the list is about eighteen lines, so the whole queue fits on about one laptop screen; what is due within the hour is open and red, the rest is a count one tap away.

Trade-off: it still lives under the card, so the card scrolls away while the list is open (at 1064 x 544 there is no room for both). Adopting it is one import: today's row, same place, same props.
