# situation 10: Grouped

- References: Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Lists and tables, inset grouped style (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/lists-and-tables, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-lists.md`); the Maps place card's "Details" groups and its "Open in Apple Maps" row as a group of its own (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps, `research/premium/situation-si/shots/hig-maps-place-card-iphone-full.png`).
- Borrowed: three rounded birch groups on the frame's grey: the route (map flush at the top, A and B rows under it), the facts as Settings rows (label left in ink, value right in grey, the blocker's value in ink 600), and the order as its own one-row group with the Orders icon.
- For the ops person: the panel reads as three separate answers (where, what is true, where to go deeper), the way an iPhone settings screen does, so the eye finds the group it needs without reading the others.
- The grey ground mirrors the side menu on the left, framing the birch card between them.
- Trade-off: groups are boxes on a tinted panel, the most "built" look of the set and a step towards boxes inside boxes; right-aligned values are slower to scan than a value column when labels vary in length.
