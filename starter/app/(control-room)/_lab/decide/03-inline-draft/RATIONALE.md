# decide 03: Inline draft

**Reference**: Zendesk auto assist, https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/7051314237466-Using-auto-assist-to-solve-tickets, shot `research/premium/product/shots/decide-01-zendesk-undo-edit.png` (board `product/decide.md` 04); Attio's agent-drafted email beside the record, https://attio.com, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-10-attio-agent-email-draft.png`; BuyOps deciding inside the expanded row, https://dribbble.com/shots/27125536-BuyOps-Procurement-SaaS, shot `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-05-buyops-inline-approve.png`.

**What it borrows**: Zendesk shows the drafted reply in place, with Edit and Approve right under it; Attio keeps the draft next to the thing it is about. Here "Send the email" opens the German text inside the card, under the question it answers, while the situation stays visible beside it. German and English are one two-way switch at the draft's top. Send folds the draft into one line, "Sending the email to Kovellin Automobilhandel", with Undo and a draining hairline.

**Why it helps**: the question, the words that answer it and the facts behind it are all on one screen, so the person checks the email against the situation without switching panels. After the decision the answer carries a tick and the next case is one row with its car, place and time.

**Trade-off**: a long email pushes the card's rows down and scrolls the headline away; the side panel's room is used for the situation instead.
