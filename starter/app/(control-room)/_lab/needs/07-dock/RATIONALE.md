# needs 07: A dock that stays while cases change

Borrows from three docked toasts on Dribbble: Untitled UI's undo with a countdown (https://dribbble.com/shots/27750801-Command-menu-Untitled-UI, `research/premium/visual/shots/decide-01-untitled-undo-countdown.png`), "Added to Night Drive / Undo" in a music app (https://dribbble.com/shots/27752078-Add-to-Playlist-Dark-Music-App-UI-Component-Music-Player-Save, `decide-02-night-drive-undo-toast.png`) and a bulk-actions dock (https://dribbble.com/shots/27259067-Bulk-Actions-Panel-System-Notifications, `decide-03-bulk-action-toasts.png`).
What it takes: a small floating bar at the bottom edge that outlives the screen under it, says the result of the last action in a full sentence, and holds the one next step; a player's mini bar whose middle opens its queue.
Here: a pill floats at the bottom of the card column with Previous, "2 of 12" and the next car with its decide-by time. It lives in Your tasks' frame, so it stays while J, K or a click change the case, and only the car name slides in. Its middle opens "Up next" upward: the next seven in order and "29 more after these". After a decision it turns dark: "Ferrari Roma decided" and "Volvo V60 next" as its one button.
Why for an ops person under time pressure: the way on is always in the same place at the same size, whatever the card above it shows, and the result of the decision is said where the eye goes next.
Trade-off: a floating element covers a strip of the column (the card gets the room back as bottom padding); dark-on-light after a decision is loud by design and should stay the only dark surface on the page.
