# situation 09: Callouts

- References: Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Maps: annotations that match the app's style, "Cluster overlapping points of interest to improve map legibility", and "In general, make your map interactive" (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps, `research/premium/situation-si/shots/hig-maps-points-of-interest-cluster.png`, text `research/premium/product/raw/chatmap/map-apple-hig.md`); Fletes, the place cards pinned beside their pins on the map (https://dribbble.com/shots/6929918-Shipment-Detail-View, `research/premium/visual/shots/situation-04-fletes-arc-route.png`).
- Borrowed: the stop names and times as callouts beside the A and B pins, so there is no separate stop list; one shared pin when both ends would overlap; zoom buttons and drag.
- For the ops person: one picture answers where, from where, and when; the facts under it are only the ones that are not on the map.
- Trade-off: callouts need room on both sides of the route, so the map is taller and two long names wrap; a map that loads late leaves the stops unread for a moment (the other variants show them as text).
