# needs 02: The next one opens by itself

Borrows from Superhuman "Achieve Inbox Zero" (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005833597709-Achieve-Inbox-Zero, `research/premium/product/shots/needs-05-superhuman-get-me-to-zero.png`) and Zendesk Play mode (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9186492658714-Using-Play-mode-to-quickly-work-through-tickets, `research/premium/product/shots/needs-07-zendesk-submit-next.png`, `needs-09-zendesk-end-of-view.png`).
What it takes: "as soon as you action an email, you'll immediately move to the next conversation" (Superhuman); Zendesk's "Next available ticket / Stay on ticket" choice and its plain message when the queue is done.
Here: after the decision the card keeps the receipt (who, when, what happens now) and under it "Volvo V60 opens in 5 s" with a hairline that fills; Open now and Stay on this case. Pointer or focus on the block pauses the count (Polaris toasts pause on hover). The last case ends in one sentence, "That was the last one. Nothing else needs you right now." Under an open card, one row says what comes after this one ("After this, Volvo V60, decide by 13:00, then 35 more"); it opens to the next six in order.
Why for an ops person under time pressure: the time between decisions goes to deciding, not to finding the next case; the count keeps the receipt readable and Stay keeps control.
Trade-off: a moving screen can surprise; five seconds and the pause are the guard. There is no undo route for a decision, so Stay is the only brake (an Undo would need the engine to hold the save).
