# welcome 03: Time left, counting down

- References: Apple HIG Live Activities, glanceable, large heavier text, animate to draw attention to an update (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities, `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-live-activities.md`); Flighty's card, the live figure first and the scheduled time under it (https://flighty.com, `research/premium/visual/shots/card-13-flighty-was-now.png`).
- Borrowed: the figure a person acts on is time left, not the clock ("30 min" big, "12:30" small under it), and the first deadline runs as a timer inside the black pill (29:41, 29:40 ...), as a Live Activity runs in the Dynamic Island.
- The room's clock starts at the server's time and moves with real time, so the minutes on every row and the timer stay true while the Overview is open.
- Why for ops: under time pressure "30 min" needs no arithmetic; the moving timer is the one live thing on the page and it is about her first decision.
- Trade-off: a ticking number pulls the eye; it appears only inside the last hour and only for the first task.
