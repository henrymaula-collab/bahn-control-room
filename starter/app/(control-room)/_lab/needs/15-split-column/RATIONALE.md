# needs 15: A list column beside the card

What it is: Apple's split view. A list column sits between the side menu and the card, always there, grouped by time under sticky names; each row has the car whole on its first line and the place and time on the second, so a narrow column never cuts the car to "Toyota RA..."; the case you are on stays highlighted and in view as J / K move you. The first group's name is level with the card's "Decide by". No row under the card.

Borrowed from: Apple HIG Sidebars: "When a data hierarchy is deeper than two levels, consider using a split view interface that includes a content list between the sidebar items and detail view" (`research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-sidebars.md`), the way Mail and Notes work. Linear's inbox, a narrow list beside the open item (https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/28becab1-031e-404d-93e2-3395cd77c4af, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-01-linear-inbox.png`).

Why it helps under time pressure: the queue is visible at every moment without a click, which suits a person who works through many tasks in a row.

Trade-off: on a 13-inch laptop the card loses about a third of its width and its headline takes four lines; at 1512 and up it reads as three calm columns.
