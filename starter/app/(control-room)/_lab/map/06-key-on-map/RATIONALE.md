# map 06: The key on the map, as switches

References: Onfleet's bottom filter bar that doubles as the legend with live counts (https://support.onfleet.com/hc/en-us/articles/8445295728916-Task-Pin-Clustering, `research/premium/product/shots/map-02-onfleet-map-and-sidebar.png`); Flexport's one-click "Exceptions" view (https://www.flexport.com/, no shot; `research/premium/product/map.md` 05); Fibery's small map style switch (https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/870d0edc-f287-4e58-a4a0-e414d6e7dda4, `research/premium/visual/shots/map-03-fibery-map-style.png`).

Borrowed: the legend lives on the map, beside the dots it explains, and each entry is a switch with its count; switching green off leaves only the exceptions. The card leads with what stopped (Flighty-style, see 08).

Why it helps: the colours are explained where the eye already is, and the welcome panel gets shorter (its own key steps aside, so nothing is said twice). An ops person can clear 272 green cars away in one click and see only the 26 that need a person and the 14 the agent holds.

Trade-off: the panel loses its quiet summary of the fleet; the switches are one more control on the first screen of the video.
