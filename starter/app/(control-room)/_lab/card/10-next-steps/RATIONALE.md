# card 10: Next steps

- References: an approval flow written as people and steps on a thin line (https://dribbble.com/shots/24444372-Approval-Workflow-Component, `research/premium/visual/shots/card-06-approval-flow-sentence.png`; the board's note: Bahn's next line can become two or three short steps on a hairline); Superhuman Auto Reminders, which promises to come back with a drafted follow-up (https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46005658551053-Auto-Reminders-Auto-Drafts, `research/premium/product/shots/card-07-superhuman-auto-drafts.png`).
- Borrowed: the hairline with one mark per step, who in grey and what in ink; the first step is the person's own (filled dot, "You, now"), the rest are the agent's promises. The shot's caps kicker and green pills are left out.
- Why for ops: the grey paragraph under today's button hides a sequence ("checks again at 10:00 and keeps the gig unpublished until ..."). Split into steps, the person sees their part ends with one press and the agent owns what follows.
- Built from the next line (split at "and then" and at a second agent verb), so it holds for any case.
- Trade-off: a split can read stiffly when the sentence was not written as steps; three steps take more height than one line.
