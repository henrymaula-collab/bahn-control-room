# needs 11: The list beside the card

What it is: "Other tasks  36 ›" opens the whole queue as the side panel, in place of the situation, exactly the way "Talk it through ›" opens the chat. It stays open while you move between cases (a click or J / K); the case you are on is highlighted and kept in view; groups by time with sticky names. Right after a decision the panel turns to the list by itself, the decided case marked "Decided". Escape or the close button brings the situation back.

Borrowed from: Apple HIG, Sheets: "Use a panel instead of a sheet if people need to repeatedly provide input and observe results" (`research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-sheets.md`), and HIG Lists: a list that drives navigation keeps its row highlighted (`research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-lists.md`). Keitoto's inbox and task detail (https://dribbble.com/shots/26058477, `research/premium/visual/shots/needs-13-keitoto-inbox-task-detail.png`): the list column carries the many tasks, the side menu only the count.

Why it helps under time pressure: the card never moves or shrinks; the list and the decision sit side by side, so working down 12 tasks is click, decide, click.

Trade-off: while the list is open the situation is one close away. It uses the panel slot the app already has, so nothing new is learned.
