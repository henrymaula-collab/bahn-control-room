# menu 03: Collapses to a rail

References: Apple HIG Sidebars, "consider letting people hide the sidebar", and its sidebar toggle glyph (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars, `research/premium/visual/shots/menu-mm-hig-sidebars.png`, raw `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hig-sidebars.md`); Zendesk's icon rail with the product mark (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9186492658714-Using-Play-mode-to-quickly-work-through-tickets, `research/premium/product/shots/card-03-zendesk-auto-assist-approve.png`).

Borrowed: the sidebar toggle at the top of the menu; Zendesk's narrow rail with the mark on top, icons only, and a count on the work icon.

Why it helps: at Henry's Safari window (1330 x 680 at 125%, 1064 CSS px) the full menu takes about a fifth of the width; the rail gives it back to the map and the decision card. It does this by itself under 1200 px and stays full above; the toggle pins either way. Names show as a tooltip on hover or focus, the person as their photo.

Trade-off: the lockup becomes the mark alone on small windows, and the names need a hover; the count badge is a small filled circle, the one badge in the app.
