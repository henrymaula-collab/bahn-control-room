# rule 01: The evidence beside the card

- Reference: Stripe Radar rule testing (https://docs.stripe.com/radar/testing, `research/premium/rule/shots/rule-06-stripe-radar-rules.png`): "Before you add or update a rule, we'll search for historical live mode payments that match the rule criteria. You can inspect that list". Apple HIG Popovers and Sheets on panels (`research/premium/rule/shots/rows-hig-sheets.png`): "use a panel if people need to repeatedly provide input and observe results".
- Borrowed: the past answers sit next to the rule, not behind it. The proposal is a card exactly like a case card (kind as the title, one question, one black button), and the panel where a case shows its situation shows the answers instead: the four people with their portraits and counts, each opening to their own answers (car, pickup place, date), the one answer that differs in Sofie's own words, the two reasons said most often, and History one tap on.
- Why for ops: Kaisa reads it the way every case reads (same place, same grammar), and the proof she needs to trust it is already on screen; the one dissent is shown, not hidden, which is what makes 29 of 30 believable.
- After "Turn it on": the line under the button says the Mercedes-Benz EQE at Arvelano Hamburg (the case of this kind waiting now) had its gig paused and left Mikko's tasks; "Turn it off" stays one tap.
- Trade-off: the most to read of the six; on a laptop the panel scrolls under "In their words".
