- Borrowed: one quiet icon that brings the map in and out; everything else as rows on hairlines, small grey section names ("Route", "Order"), places left and times right in tabular figures; the reference itself is the link to the order.
- For the ops person who has seen this kind of case a hundred times: the whole order fits on one screen with no scrolling at Henry's Safari size, and the eye runs down one column of values.
- The blocker leads the order facts in 600; the distance row says "Both in Hamburg, 0.3 km" where a map would only show two touching pins.
- Trade-off: no map by default and a link that is a reference rather than words; a newcomer may not guess that "CD-420458 ›" opens the order.
