# card 04: Why this

- Reference: Linear Triage Intelligence (https://linear.app/docs/triage-intelligence, `research/premium/product/shots/card-01-linear-suggestion-why.png`, `card-02-linear-triage-intel.png`): a suggestion with a popover "Why this assignee was suggested", two lines of evidence, "Alternatives", then one Accept.
- Borrowed: the popover's order (why, then the alternative, then accept) and its size: two pieces of evidence, not a dossier. Here the evidence is the case's first-hand facts (the pickup place on the call, with who and when) and the kind's record in one sentence ("Nobody has decided a seller hold before"), fetched only when opened.
- Why for ops: the expert answers from the card and never opens it; the doubter gets the agent's proof without leaving the decision, and can take the alternative from the same place.
- The card itself stays today's, so this is the cheapest change to adopt: one quiet text button.
- Trade-off: a popover is one more layer; the record sentence can show that few people chose the suggestion (Volvo: 8 of 53), which is honest but may slow the decision.
