# rows 01: A grey line in every row

- Reference: Okta getting started (https://nicelydone.club/examples/screens/b7b9dc19-cae1-43bd-8df4-bba419ab0112, `research/premium/visual/shots/rows-03-okta-getting-started.png`): every row has a title and one grey sentence. Partnership details, collapsible sections (https://dribbble.com/shots/26339595-Partnership-Details, `research/premium/visual/shots/rows-07-partnership-collapsible.png`): one grey line under each section title says what is inside.
- Borrowed: the grey second line on every row, not only on Talk it through. Why it came to you reads "Seller hold on the car, needs the customer"; Other tasks names the next one ("BMW M4 next, 12:30", red within the hour) with the count on the right.
- Many tasks: opened, Other tasks shows five, then "Show all 36"; an opened row scrolls itself into view (on a laptop the rows start near the fold).
- Why for ops: the expert reads the three lines and decides without opening anything; the newcomer knows which row holds what before tapping.
- Trade-off: one more line per row (about 20 px each); the why line repeats two of the three facts the row opens to. With `?v=needs:nn` the needs variant's row takes Other tasks' place.
