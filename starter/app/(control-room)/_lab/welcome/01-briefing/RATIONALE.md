- The sentence is computed: "Three cars need you by 13:00." (all today), "12 cars need you, the first by 12:25." (many), "Nothing needs you. The agent is on 14." (none).
- Why for ops: the count, the deadline and the next step are read in one glance; the pill no longer repeats the count the sentence already says.
- Many: the rows stop where the panel would leave the frame and the rest is one row ("8 more in Your tasks"), so the key never falls off at Henry's Safari size.
- Trade-off: "Bahn Express" leaves the headline; the brand is still the face and the pill.
