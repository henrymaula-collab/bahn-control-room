# card 09: Two answers

- References: HEY's Screener (https://www.hey.com/features/the-screener/, raw `research/premium/product/raw/pp/hey-screener.md`): a first-time sender is one yes-or-no with two equal answers, reversible later; Stripe Radar's closing actions, each written with its consequence ("Approve closes the review with no changes", "A completed refund is permanent"; https://docs.stripe.com/radar/reviews); the equal-width approve and reject pair (https://dribbble.com/shots/27816375, `research/premium/visual/shots/card-09-shift-approve-reject.png`).
- Borrowed: the two likely answers side by side at the same width, each with its first consequence written under it ("Nothing is sent."), the suggestion filled and the other the soft secondary; a third option behind "One more option".
- Why for ops: most cases are really "do what the agent says, or hold". Putting the hold beside the default, with what each does, answers "what if I don't?" without opening anything.
- The second answer still opens the sheet on its reason step, so the shift lead learns why the agent was overruled.
- Trade-off: two buttons of equal width weaken the default a little (Apple keeps one prominent button); long consequences wrap to three lines in a narrow window.
